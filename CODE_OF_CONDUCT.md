# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone,
regardless of experience, background, or identity. Everyone interacting in the
project — issues, pull requests, discussions — is expected to act respectfully
and in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions) and to public spaces when an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove,
edit, or reject contributions, comments, or contributors that violate them.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub
(**Security → Advisories → Report a vulnerability**, or by contacting the
maintainers). Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially and fairly.
